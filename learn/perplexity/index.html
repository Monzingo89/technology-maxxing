Perplexity

web-grounded answers and research with source links

AI research and answer platform · Perplexity

Why learn Perplexity?

Helps collect and synthesize information with links back to sources. A citation is evidence to inspect, not proof that an answer is correct. Grok and Claude are comparison links; HTTP connects its developer services to applications.

The problem it solves

Reduces the work of locating and synthesizing source material for research questions.

Where it came from

Perplexity combines search and generated answers in a research-oriented product. Its developer platform separately exposes search and agent-related APIs. The user-facing service and its developer interfaces are related but not identical offerings.

Build your understanding

Work through these exercises in order. Try the task before opening a reference.

  1. Ask a research question and open every cited source.
  2. Separate primary sources from secondary commentary.
  3. Create a claim-to-source table and flag unsupported statements.
  4. Repeat a query with a date boundary and compare the evidence.
  5. Produce a short research memo with uncertainty and contradictory evidence recorded.
