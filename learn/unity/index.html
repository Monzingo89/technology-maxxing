Unity

cross-platform real-time 3d app engine

2026 · Daily Agent

Why learn Unity?

Unity helps teams build and ship interactive 2D/3D apps from one codebase across mobile, desktop, web, and console, while standardizing collaboration, automated builds, and release pipelines.

Where it came from

2005: Unity first launched, initially focused on Mac game development. 2010s: Strong mobile support and Asset Store growth made Unity a mainstream engine. Plastic SCM became part of Unity and is now branded as Unity Version Control. 2023-2024: Pricing policy controversy led to revised terms and stronger trust messaging. 2024: Unity 6 branding replaced year-based major naming for the engine line.
